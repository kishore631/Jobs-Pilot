## 📌 About the Project

**Jobs Pilot** is an independent job information platform designed to help job seekers discover employment opportunities, explore career guidance, and access application links.

Our goal is to make job searching simpler by presenting relevant job information in an accessible, user-friendly format.

## 🎯 Main Objectives

- 💼 Help job seekers discover employment opportunities.
- 🎓 Share job openings for freshers and experienced candidates.
- 📍 Display job locations, eligibility criteria, and experience requirements.
- 💰 Share salary information when it is available and verified.
- 🔗 Direct candidates to relevant employer and application websites.
- 📚 Provide career guidance and job-search resources.

## 📝 Managing Job Listings

Job listings are maintained manually in `jobs.js` through the `window.JOBS_PILOT_JOBS` array.

Before publishing a vacancy:

- ✅ Verify the details using a reliable source, preferably the employer's official careers page.
- 🏢 Confirm the company and job title.
- 🎓 Check eligibility and experience requirements.
- 💰 Publish only verified salary information.
- 📅 Confirm application deadlines and job availability.
- 🔗 Check that the application link works.
- ❌ Unpublish or remove expired vacancies.

Only publish genuine, currently available opportunities. Use **Not disclosed** when salary information is unavailable.

## 🔍 SEO and Search Engine Visibility

Jobs Pilot includes basic search-engine discovery files:

- 🤖 `robots.txt` — provides crawling instructions.
- 🗺️ `sitemap.xml` — lists website URLs for discovery.
- 📈 **Google Search Console** — can be used to monitor indexing and search performance.

### 💡 Google Job Search Rich Results

To pursue Google's job search rich results, individual job pages should have unique, crawlable URLs, complete visible job details, valid `JobPosting` structured data, and accurate expiry handling.

Structured data must match the visible job description. Do not mark up unverified or expired vacancies.

## 🔒 Privacy, Advertising, and Website Policies

Jobs Pilot includes privacy, terms, and disclaimer pages. Review these pages to ensure they accurately describe the website's actual practices.

If analytics, cookies, advertising, or other third-party services are enabled:

- 🛡️ Update the privacy policy accordingly.
- 📋 Meet applicable consent and disclosure requirements.
- 📖 Follow relevant Google publisher policies before using AdSense.

**AdSense approval is not guaranteed.** Content quality, website functionality, policy compliance, and other eligibility requirements matter.

🚫 Never click your own ads, encourage users to click ads, disguise advertisements as job listings, or place ads where accidental clicks are likely.

## ⚠️ Important Disclaimer

Jobs Pilot is an independent job information platform. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, it is not affiliated with or endorsed by the employers whose vacancies are shared.

Job information, eligibility, salaries, deadlines, and availability may change. Candidates should verify details through the relevant employer or official application source before applying.

Jobs Pilot does not guarantee employment or selection.
