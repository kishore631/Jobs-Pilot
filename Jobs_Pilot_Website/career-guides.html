CAREER TOOLKIT

Build skills for your next opportunity.

Practical starting points for a more organized job search. Review, adapt and apply these ideas to your own situation.

GUIDE 01

Build a clear, focused resume

Make it easy for a recruiter to understand your skills, education, projects and relevant experience.

Try this

  • Keep contact details accurate and professional.
  • Tailor the skills section to the actual job requirements.
  • Describe projects with your contribution, tools and measurable outcomes when truthful.
  • Check spelling, links and formatting before sending.
GUIDE 02

Prepare for interviews

Preparation helps you explain your experience and reason through unfamiliar questions.

Try this

  • Read the job description and research the employer.
  • Prepare concise examples of challenges and what you learned.
  • Practice core technical or role-specific topics.
  • Prepare thoughtful questions about the role and team.
GUIDE 03

Search for fresher opportunities

Organize applications so you can follow up and learn from the process.

Try this

  • Track the role, source, date applied and current status.
  • Check eligibility and location before applying.
  • Use official employer career pages to verify listings.
  • Set a realistic weekly application and learning target.
GUIDE 04

Spot suspicious job offers

Pause and verify when an opportunity asks for money or sensitive information unexpectedly.

Warning signs

  • Payment demanded to guarantee employment.
  • Unofficial email addresses impersonating a company.
  • Pressure to share passwords or one-time passcodes.
  • Vague job details or unrealistic promises.
GUIDE 05

Plan your skill development

Use job descriptions to identify practical skills to build next.

Try this

  • Identify recurring skills in roles you want.
  • Choose a small project that demonstrates one skill.
  • Document what you built and what you learned.
  • Be honest about your level of experience.
GUIDE 06

Apply thoughtfully

Focus on roles that match your skills and follow each employer's instructions.

Try this

  • Use the requested resume format and application channel.
  • Provide accurate information.
  • Keep a copy of the job description for reference.
  • Never pay to apply unless a legitimate, clearly disclosed process is independently verified; ordinary job applications should not require payment.
