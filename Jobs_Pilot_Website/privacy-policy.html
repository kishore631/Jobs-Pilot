Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jobs Pilot (“we”, “us” or “our”) may collect, use and disclose information when you visit this website. Please update this policy if the site's features, service providers or data practices change.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email or through a contact feature, we may receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, name and message. Please do not send passwords, financial account details or other sensitive information.

Information collected automatically

Like many websites, hosting providers and security tools may process technical information such as browser type, device information, pages requested, approximate location inferred from network information, timestamps and diagnostic logs. The exact information depends on the services configured for this website.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website may use essential technologies needed for functionality, security or preferences. If analytics, advertising or other third-party technologies are enabled, they may use cookies or similar identifiers according to their own policies and your applicable choices.

Advertising, including Google AdSense

If we enable Google AdSense or other advertising services, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve or measure ads based on visits to this website or other websites, subject to their settings and applicable requirements. Where required, we will provide appropriate notice and obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or personalized advertising technologies. You can learn more at Google's advertising technologies information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Do not state that advertising or analytics are active unless they have actually been configured. When you add a service, update this policy to accurately describe it.

How information may be used

Information may be used to operate and secure the website, respond to messages, improve content and usability, diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

Sharing and external links

We may rely on hosting, security, analytics or advertising providers if configured. We do not control third-party websites linked from job listings or resources. When you visit an external site, review its privacy policy and terms.

Retention and security

We retain messages and technical records only as reasonably needed for their purpose, subject to legal or operational requirements. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict certain personal information. You may also manage browser cookies and applicable advertising preferences. Contact us to make a privacy request.

Children

This website is intended for a general job-seeking audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Please contact us if you believe a child has sent us personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or applicable requirements change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
